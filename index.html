Rhythm Guide

Kodály Terminology

  • ta: 1 Beat Quarter Note (Single Sound)
  • ti-ti: 1 Beat Pair of Eighth Notes (Dual Sounds)
  • ta rest: 1 Beat Silent Space
  • ta-a: 2 Beat Half Note (Sustained Tone)
  • tika-tika: 1 Beat Four Sixteenth Notes
