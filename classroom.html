Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 2026

Solfaic operates completely locally within your client-side browser runtime environment. We do not utilise background trackers, cookies, or telemetry scripts.

Your interactive gameplay telemetry, including level tracking, streaks, and validation scoring parameters, is stored exclusively in active session memory profiles. No data is transmitted to external cloud servers or database-backed third parties.

Audio processing tasks are handled entirely through your native local hardware via the Web Audio API and Tone.js libraries.
